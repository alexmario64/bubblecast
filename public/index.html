        Text to speech

        Read chat aloud on stream with your computer’s own voices.

        Current voice
        DefaultYour computer’s voice

        How viewers trigger it

        Viewers type the command and their message, for example “!tts hello everyone”. It works on Twitch, YouTube, TikTok and Kick.

        Make a Twitch reward that asks viewers for text and type its name here. Bubblecast reads what they write. Kick doesn’t share channel point rewards with Bubblecast yet.

        Connect Twitch as the channel owner in Connections so Bubblecast receives channel point redemptions.

        Where and who
        Platforms
        Who can use the command

        You can always use it. Each platform’s own moderators, VIPs, subscribers and members are recognized from their badges.

        Voice
        Choose a voice

        Natural voices sound far more human. Download one once, then pick it.

        Limits and bad words

        Common swear words and slurs in English and Spanish are always caught, even with tricks like symbols (f*ck), numbers (sh1t), spaced-out letters or look-alike characters. Offensive usernames aren’t read either.

        Add the voice to OBS
        /tts

        In OBS, add a Browser source with this URL and tick “Control audio via OBS”. It shows nothing on screen and gets its own volume slider.
